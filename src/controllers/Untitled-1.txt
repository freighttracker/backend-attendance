 TOP 25 MOST PROBABLE QUESTIONS 
========================================================================
========
1. Rajasthan Budget 2026-27 was presented by whom and on which date?
→ Deputy CM Diya Kumari, 11 February 2026
2. What is the projected GSDP of Rajasthan for 2026-27?
→ ₹21,52,100 crore (15% growth)
3. What is the total expenditure in Rajasthan Budget 2026-27?
→ ₹6,10,956 crore
4. Fiscal deficit target for Rajasthan 2026-27?
→ 3.7% of GSDP (₹79,493 crore)
5. How many districts are currently in Rajasthan after Dec 2024
reorganization?
→ 41 districts (8 retained out of 19 new, 9 abolished)
6. Name the 8 retained new districts of Rajasthan.
→ Balotra, Beawar, Deeg, Didwana-Kuchaman, Kotputli-Behror,
Khairthal-Tijara, Phalodi, Salumber
7. Pachpadra Refinery capacity and inauguration date?
→ 9 MMTPA refining + 2.4 MMTPA petrochemical; inaugurated 4 July 2026
8. What is the full form of HRRL and its ownership pattern?
→ HPCL Rajasthan Refinery Limited; HPCL 74%, Rajasthan Govt 26%
Page 2
9. Ramjal Setu Link Project (formerly ERCP) benefits how many districts?
→ 17 districts of Eastern Rajasthan
10. Estimated cost of Ramjal Setu Link Project?
→ ₹72,000 crore (90% Centre, 10% State)
11. Yamuna Water Agreement pipeline length and cost for Shekhawati?
→ 265 km underground pipeline, ₹32,000 crore
12. How many GI Tags does Rajasthan have as of Jan 2026?
→ 23 GI Tags (latest: Nagauri Ashwagandha from Nagaur)
13. What is Mukhyamantri Yuva Swarojgar Yojana?
→ Self-employment for 1 lakh youth, 100% interest subsidy up to ₹10 lakh
14. Rajasthan's target for solar pump installation under PM-KUSUM?
→ 50,000 solar pumps (₹400.16 crore allocated)
15. What is the loan limit under Lakhpati Didi scheme after Budget
2026-27?
→ Increased from ₹1 lakh to ₹1.5 lakh
16. Name the sports university announced in Rajasthan Budget.
→ Maharana Pratap Sports University, Achrol (Jaipur)
17. What is Rajasthan State Testing Agency (RSTA)?
→ On lines of NTA for transparent competitive exams
18. Total road length approved under 77 road projects in June 2026?
→ 686.42 km across 33 districts (₹676.74 crore)
19. What is the capacity of SJVN's Bikaner Solar Project commissioned in
Dec 2025?
→ 1,000 MW (₹5,492 crore, 5,000 acres)
20. How many Anganwadi centres will get "Jadui Pitara" under Budget
2026-27?
→ 22,748 centres (₹323 crore)
21. What is the tourism allocation in Rajasthan Budget 2026-27?
→ ₹5,000 crore (theme: "Vikas ki Virasat bhi")
22. How many new handpumps and tubewell connections announced?
→ 20,000 handpumps + 3 lakh tubewell connections
23. What is the Green Budget allocation for 2026-27?
→ ₹33,476 crore (20.81% increase)
Page 3
24. Under Jal Jeevan Mission, how many households got tap connections?
→ 62.40 lakh households (target: every rural household by 2028)
25. What is the name of Rajasthan's anti-drug campaign?
→ "Rajbhavey" (to make Rajasthan drug-free)
